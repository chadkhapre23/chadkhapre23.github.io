ABOUT JAMES NYARANGO TV

Our identity, our purpose and our commitment to responsible journalism.

A Voice for News, Information and Public Interest

James Nyarango TV (JNTV) is a digital media and broadcasting platform focused on delivering news, current affairs, information and entertainment to audiences in Uganda, Africa and around the world.

Through news reporting, digital publishing, interviews and video programming, JNTV seeks to make important developments accessible, understandable and relevant to the public.

Our identity is guided by one central promise: INFORM • ENTERTAIN • EMPOWER.

Our Mission and Vision

Our Mission

To provide timely, relevant and accessible news, information and entertainment through responsible journalism, digital media and engaging storytelling.

Our Vision

To become a trusted and influential media platform connecting audiences with important stories, ideas and developments across Uganda, Africa and the world.

Our Editorial Values

The principles that guide our approach to news and public communication.

01

Accuracy

We value factual reporting, careful verification and responsible presentation of information.

02

Integrity

We seek to communicate honestly, avoid misleading information and act responsibly.

03

Public Interest

We focus on stories and issues that matter to communities, institutions and society.

04

Fairness

We value balanced reporting and the fair representation of relevant perspectives.

05

Professionalism

We aim for high standards in journalism, communication, presentation and media production.

06

Innovation

We embrace digital platforms and new ways of sharing information with audiences.

Our Editorial Commitment

JNTV recognises the importance of accuracy, verification, fairness and accountability in journalism. We aim to distinguish factual reporting from opinion and commentary, correct significant errors where identified, and treat people and communities with dignity and respect.
