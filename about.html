ABOUT JAMES NYARANGO TV

Informing. Entertaining. Empowering.

Who We Are

James Nyarango TV (JNTV) is a digital media and broadcasting platform committed to delivering news, current affairs, politics, business, sports, entertainment and other important developments from Uganda, Africa and around the world.

We use digital media to connect audiences with information, stories and developments that matter. Our platform seeks to provide accessible news coverage and engaging media content for audiences across different communities.

Our Mission

To inform, entertain and empower audiences through responsible journalism, reliable information, engaging programmes and innovative digital broadcasting.

Our Vision

To become a recognised and trusted digital media platform connecting communities through quality journalism, creativity and meaningful storytelling.

Our Core Values

Integrity

We value honesty, accuracy, fairness and responsibility in our communication.

Professionalism

We are committed to quality content, responsible reporting and professional media standards.

Innovation

We embrace digital technology and creativity to improve how audiences access information.

What We Cover

Our coverage includes breaking news, national and international affairs, politics, business and economy, sports, entertainment, interviews, special reports and digital video productions.
